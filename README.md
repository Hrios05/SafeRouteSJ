# SafeRouteSJ
# 🧑‍🧑‍🧒 Who We Are
SafeRoute is designed by three San Jose State Students: Alisha Pol, Hector Rios, and Vaishnavi Panchal (Team #20). We chose the theme of Public Safety.

# 💡 Inspiration
SafeRoute was born from our own experiences as young adults living in downtown San Jose. At just 19 years old, we often felt unsafe walking alone at night or through unfamiliar areas.

What should have been simple walks turned into moments of anxiety and caution. We envisioned a tool that uses real-time data, community reports, and smart tech to help people travel with confidence.

With public records from the San Jose Police Department, we analyzed trends and crime locations to build SafeRoute’s smart routing system based on real reports, not assumptions.

SafeRoute isn’t just an app. It’s a mission to make neighborhoods feel safer, starting with our own community.

# 📍 What It Does
SafeRoute is designed to provide users with the safest route based on submitted crime reports and San Jose datasets. It is intended to calculate the safest, not just the fastest route.

# 🛠️ How We Built It
My team and I implemented Firebase/Firestore Database, Google Maps APIs, React with Byte React, and TypeScript.

# 🧱 Challenges We Ran Into
Our main challenge was figuring the most efficient way to implement the Google Maps APIs. Through teamwork and careful consideration of best practices, we were able to overcome this hurdle, allowing for the remainder of our development process to be successful.

# 🏆 Accomplishments That We're Proud Of
For many of my team members, this was our first time developing a full-stack website. Although it was intimidating at first, by breaking down the features into smaller subsets, we were able to make our ideas come to life despite the limited time frame.

# 📚 What We Learned
From this experience, we learned just how important teamwork is. Not only do your partners hold you accountable, but they also provide tremendous support in guiding you. Additionally, we recognized the importance of considering the experiences of others in order to provide safe, innovative solutions for our community.

# ⏭️ What's Next For SafeRoute
In regards to what's next for SafeRoute, we plan on implementing a mobile app version of our idea, allowing for a more efficient and convenient way to protect others.
